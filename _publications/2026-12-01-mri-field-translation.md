**Work in Progress.**

- We present a semi-supervised, frequency-aware, attention-guided framework for synthesizing 3T MRI from 64mT ultra-low-field scans, addressing paired-data scarcity through adversarial refinement.
- Our method preserves anatomical fidelity while restoring high-frequency 3T textures, outperforming recent baselines across T1, T2, and FLAIR contrasts with an effective perception-distortion tradeoff.
